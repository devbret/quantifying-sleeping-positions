# Quantifying Sleep Positions With Body Weight

![Photo of various electronic components.](https://hosting.photobucket.com/bbcfb0d4-be20-44a0-94dc-65bff8947cf2/5b93a2c3-8297-4a47-996c-a428c35c57b8.jpg)

Quantifying sleep behavior by capturing and analyzing how body weight is distributed across a sleeping surface over extended periods of time.

## Application Overview

Delves into self-quantification by mapping the physicality of sleep through precise measurements of body weight distribution. At its core, the project employs a Raspberry Pi 5 computer paired with eight force-sensitive resistor (FSR) pads. These FSRs will be strategically positioned across my bed to capture real-time pressure data.

The system then leverages pressure signatures generated by different body positions. By continuously monitoring these signals throughout the night, the setup can infer whether I am lying on my side, back, stomach or a combination. Additionally, this project tracks the frequency and duration of shifts between positions, providing insights into night time behavior overlooked in traditional sleep tracking.

The motivation behind this project is to understand how my body interacts with its environment during rest. And will thus transform raw pressure data into actionable insights, such as identifying dominant sleep positions, patterns of restlessness or correlations between posture and sleep quality.

### Technical Changes

Initially an alternative approach was explored using an AMG8833 infrared thermal sensor, which promised to detect body heat signatures. However, the build phase presented significant challenges, especially with hardware assembly and signal detection. Soldering delicate components, calibrating sensors and reducing noise proved more complex than anticipated.

FSRs provide direct measurements of physical weight distribution, making them less susceptible to inaccuracies introduced by thermal metrics. The shift also simplified hardware design. Beyond technical feasibility, pressure sensing aligns more closely with the project’s core objective of capturing interactions between my body and bed.

### Data Pipeline and Future Visualization

A Python script will continuously query the FSR pads via GPIO pins at regular intervals, capturing pressure readings across all eight sensors. These raw values are then ingested by a Flask API. Collected data is stored in JSON format, enabling integration with custom-built analytics tools.

Visualization will play a critical role in translating raw pressure readings into insights. Using D3.js and JavaScript, I plan to develop interactive web graphics to represent body weight distribution over time.

The ultimate goal is to collect data over a 30 to 90 day period, allowing me to identify recurring patterns in nocturnal behavior which might correlate with factors like fatigue, discomfort or sleep disorders. This long-term study will not only deepen my understanding of personal sleep habits but also serve as a foundation for further research questions
